# To: The National Archives, Kew
**Re: Records of a Royal Engineers officer commissioned 1776 — routes to parentage**

Subject line: *Board of Ordnance records: identifying the parentage of an RE officer commissioned 1776*

**Send to:** TNA's online contact form. **Note:** TNA does **not** do free research beyond brief
signposting; for anything substantial they will refer you to their paid record copying service or to
an independent researcher list. So this letter is deliberately framed as a **signposting** question —
which series, not "please look him up".

---

Dear Enquiries Team,

I am building a small non-commercial family archive and would be grateful for signposting rather
than research.

My subject is **Robert D'Arcy** (died Chatham, 13 May 1827; buried St Mary, Chatham, 19 May 1827,
aged 76), an officer of the **Corps of Royal Engineers**. His career is fully documented in Connolly
and Edwards, *Roll of Officers of the Corps of Royal Engineers from 1660 to 1898*, entry 203: second
lieutenant **17 January 1776** with no prior ensigncy, through to major-general 12 August 1819, C.B.

What I have been unable to establish is **his parentage**, and I would like advice on which series
are most likely to carry it. I have already ruled out or exhausted:

- **WO 149** (Royal Military Academy Woolwich, Registers of Cadets) — the catalogue gives 1790–1939,
  and the volumes are held at Sandhurst, so a cadet of the early 1770s falls outside it.
- Indexed parish baptisms across England, c. 1741–1761 — no candidate.
- The *Gentleman's Magazine*, May to July 1827 — no obituary.
- Connolly's *Roll* itself, which records no parentage for anyone.

My specific questions:

1. For a man commissioned into the Royal Engineers in **January 1776**, what is the surviving
   documentation of his **admission and commissioning** — and in which series? I understand Board of
   Ordnance officers were administered separately from the War Office until 1855. Are the
   **Board of Ordnance minutes (WO 47)** or **Ordnance Office registers (WO 54)** the right place to
   look for a cadet warrant or commission entry of that date, and do such entries typically name a
   father, guardian or recommender?
2. Do any surviving **Royal Military Academy, Woolwich admission papers pre-dating WO 149** exist at
   Kew under Ordnance references?
3. Is there a **half-pay, full-pay or pension record** for a Royal Engineers major-general of 1819–27
   that would give a birth date or place? I have three irreconcilable indications of his age.
4. Is his **will or administration** likely to be in PROB 11 (he died 13 May 1827 at Chatham), and if
   so is it searchable by name in Discovery?

I am content to pay for copies, and to engage an independent researcher if that is the appropriate
route — a steer on which series would let me commission that work precisely rather than speculatively.

The archive is public and free: **https://daviddef.github.io/TheDArcy/**
