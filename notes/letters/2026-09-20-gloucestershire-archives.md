Dear Gloucestershire Archives,

Andrew Parry kindly replied to an earlier enquiry of mine on 14 September, explaining that you do
not offer a research service and pointing me to the Gloucestershire Family History Society and to
the filmed registers on Ancestry. I have taken both suggestions up, and I am not asking you to
search for anything here.

I am writing instead about **copying, and about what your finding aids cover** — four references,
in the order they matter to me. I am in Australia and cannot visit. This is for a free public
family-history archive with no commercial purpose; I am happy to pay ordinary reprographic fees
and to observe any conditions on reproduction.

## 1. Gloucester diocesan marriage allegations and bonds, 1795–1799

This is the one that matters most, and my question is about the finding aid rather than the
documents.

I am looking for the marriage of a **JOHN SANIGER** to a **LYDIA** — surname unknown, which is the
point — somewhere around 1796 or 1797. Their eldest recorded child was baptised at Berkeley in
1798. I have now established that no marriage of anyone of this surname, under eight spellings, is
indexed anywhere in England between 1790 and 1802 in the FamilySearch transcription, and that both
Berkeley and Wotton-under-Edge are well covered by that index for those years. So either they
married elsewhere, or the entry is not in that transcription.

If they married by licence, the allegation and bond would survive in the GDR series and would name
her, her parish and very often her father.

**My questions:** Is there a **name index** to the Gloucester diocesan marriage allegations and
bonds for the 1790s — in the searchroom, in print, or online? If there is, is it something I could
consult or buy a copy of from here? And if there is not, roughly how much material does one year
of allegations amount to, so that I can judge whether asking someone to go through five years of it
is a reasonable thing to commission?

## 2. GDR/B4/1/387 — Christian Saniger v. Mary Millard, defamation, 1768

A consistory court cause. I should be grateful for **a quote to copy it in full**, including any
depositions. If the file is large, I would rather have a note of its extent first than a surprise.

My interest is that church-court depositions give the age, abode and occupation of every witness,
and a Saniger litigating in 1768 stands one generation above the John Saniger above.

## 3. D5555/2/9 — transcripts of Men and Armour 1608, the hearth taxes of 1664 and 1672, and the poll tax of 1678

I should be grateful for **a quote to copy the whole bundle**, or, if that is impractical, for
whatever part of it covers **Berkeley hundred**.

I have read the 1608 muster in Maclean's printed edition, and I am aware that edition carries
misreadings — Gloucestershire Notes and Queries listed several when it appeared, including one that
affects my own surname directly. A modern transcript made from the manuscript is exactly what I
need to check it against.

## 4. D2957/41 — the Berkeley estate deeds

Here my question is simply **what this reference consists of**. The catalogue descriptions I can
see from here mention Saniger mortgages of 1692, 1709 and 1716 and a conveyance of 1791, which
suggests a substantial run rather than a single item.

**Is there an item-level list** for D2957/41 that could be sent or consulted remotely? I am trying
to establish whether a conveyance of Saniger Farm from an Edward Saniger to the Earl of Berkeley,
which a published article of 1882 dates to 1774, is among them. Knowing what the series contains
would tell me whether that is a copying order or a job for a researcher.

## And one last thing

If you keep a **list of independent researchers** who work in your searchroom, I should be grateful
for it. Several of the questions above are plainly jobs for a person on the spot rather than for a
copying department, and I would rather commission that properly than keep asking you for things you
have already told me you cannot do.

Thank you for your time, and please pass on my thanks to Mr Parry for a prompt and useful answer
last week.

With best wishes,
David Defranceski
david.defranceski@gmail.com

## Notes for David before sending

- **This deliberately asks for nothing they have refused.** Item 1 asks about a finding aid, item 4
  asks what a reference contains, and items 2 and 3 are copy quotes against exact references. That
  is the whole of what an archive with no research service can still supply.
- **Item 1 is the live one.** It is the only route left to Lydia's maiden name now that the
  registers and the index have both been exhausted — see `/hannah-baptism/`.
- **Two things here are new this week** and did not exist when the 14 September enquiry was sent:
  the controlled finding that no Saniger marries anywhere in England 1790–1802, and the
  Discovery API sweep that turned up D654/III/9 ("Oldminsters in Swanhanger"). I have left
  D654/III/9 **out** of this letter to keep it to four asks; it can go in a second one.
- **The researcher list at the end is the highest-value ask** if the copying answers disappoint.
