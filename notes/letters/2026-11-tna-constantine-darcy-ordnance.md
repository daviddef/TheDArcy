- WO 55/2281 (Ordnance Office, military surveyors): *Constantine D'Arcy, appointed vice Strickland, p 48*; and

- A Royal Artillery officer Constantine D'Arcy: second lieutenant **19 August 1778** (read from the order of Kane's 1869
  list, not printed against his name; after 19 September 1777 on the printed Army List of 1778 alone), first lieutenant
  **12 July 1780**, died at Grenada **18 March 1794**.

**Not claimed anywhere:** that the surveyor of 1775 and the artillery officer are one man (a forename and the Ordnance
is all that joins them), that either is the tree's Constantine, or that either is anyone's son. The catalogue
descriptions are samples, so the volumes themselves may say more or less than the descriptions do.

Dear Enquiries Team,

I am building a small non-commercial family archive and would be grateful for signposting and, if possible, a quote for
copies. I am not asking you to research or to identify anyone.

**1. Two catalogue entries I would like to see in full.** Discovery describes **WO 55/2281** (Ordnance Office, military
surveyors) as listing "Constantine D'Arcy. Appointed vice Strickland, p 48" and "Charles D'Arcy. Promoted to Engineers
[Royal Engineers ?], p 53", and **WO 46/10** (Board of Ordnance, out-letters and papers, 1775–1777) as listing
military surveyors by name, appointment date and the profession of each one's parent. Could you tell me:

- whether pages 48 and 53 of WO 55/2281, and the entry for D'Arcy in WO 46/10, can be copied, and what a quote for
  copies of those entries would be (a scan or photocopy of the pages is enough);
- whether the volumes are produced as originals, or are available only as microfilm or in another form.

**2. Where the commission of a Royal Artillery second lieutenant of August 1778 would be recorded.** I understand Board
of Ordnance officers were administered separately from the War Office until 1855. For a man commissioned into the
Royal Regiment of Artillery in 1778 and promoted first lieutenant in July 1780:

- which series holds the **commission or warrant entries** for that date (I have seen WO 54 suggested for Royal
  Engineers commissions of 1776; is that the right series for Artillery as well?), and is there a finding aid that
  gives volume and folio by date, so that I can ask for the right pages and not a whole volume;
- whether such entries ever give an age, a place of birth, a father, or a recommender;
- whether any muster, pay or return survives that would place an Artillery officer on **Grenada in 1794**, and
  where.

**3. Wills.** I have searched PROB 11 for 1794–1800 by name and found no will for a Constantine D'Arcy (the same
search finds the will of a Robert D'Arcy, so it reads the index). Is there another probate court or series you would
point me to for an officer who died at Grenada in March 1794?

I am content to pay for copies and to engage an independent researcher from your list if that is the right route; a steer
on which series and which pages would let me commission that work precisely.

The archive is public and free: **https://darcy.defranceski.com/** (the page for him is
https://darcy.defranceski.com/constantine/).
