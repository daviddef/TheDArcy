# To: National Archives of Australia
**Re: Digitisation of AIF service dossier B2455, D'ARCY L A (SERN 11335)**

Subject line: *Digitisation request — B2455 D'ARCY L A, service number 11335*

**Send to:** the NAA via RecordSearch (`recordsearch.naa.gov.au`) or their contact form.
**Note:** NAA digitises First World War service dossiers **on request and free of charge** where
they are not already online. This one is very likely to succeed and to cost nothing; it may take
several weeks. **Check RecordSearch first — it may already be digitised**, in which case no letter
is needed at all.

Dear National Archives of Australia,

I should like to request the digitisation of a First World War service dossier, if it is not already
available online.

| Rank | Sergeant |
| Unit | 12th Australian Depot Unit of Supply, Australian Army Service Corps |
| Place of birth | South Brisbane, Queensland |

| Next of kin | his wife, Ivy Miriam D'Arcy |
| Embarked | HMAT *Demosthenes* A64 |
| Returned to Australia | 7 July 1919 |

He was my wife's great-grandfather. The family has always known only that he "served with the
Australian Forces in France during the Great War" — the phrase used in his obituary in the
*Courier-Mail* of 11 November 1936 — and the dossier is the only surviving account of what he
actually did. He died at forty-four, in 1936, leaving five children, and none of his descendants has
seen it.

**Please note the spelling.** He appears in Australian War Memorial records under **both** *Lindesay
Atkinson D'Arcy* and *Lindsay Darcy*, so the dossier may be catalogued either way.

If the item is already digitised, please simply point me to it and disregard this request.
