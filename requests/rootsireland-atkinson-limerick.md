# Request — Roots Ireland: Richard Atkinson and Anne, of Limerick

**What it is:** `rootsireland.ie`, the Irish Family History Foundation's transcription database.
Subscription or pay-per-view. The Limerick Genealogy centre's holdings are indexed there.

**Why it matters:** **Richard Atkinson and Anne** are the archive's deepest Irish ancestors and we
know literally nothing about them but their names — recovered from a single line in their son's
christening.

The son is **Paul Cole Atkinson**, christened **9 July 1832 at St Mary's Cathedral, Limerick**, who
emigrated to Brisbane on **2 September 1862**, installed the first organ at All Saints' Wickham
Terrace in 1864, and died at South Brisbane in 1906.

## The searches

**1. Other children of Richard Atkinson and Anne.**
Baptisms, county **Limerick**, father **Richard Atkinson**, years **1820–1845**. Leave the mother's
name blank at first, then repeat with mother **Anne**. Siblings give you the family's parish,
Richard's occupation, and often an address — and each new baptism is another chance at a townland.

**2. The marriage of Richard Atkinson and Anne.**
Marriages, county **Limerick**, **1815–1832**. Her surname is unknown, which is exactly what this
search is for.

**3. Jane Creech** — Paul Cole Atkinson's wife, born about 1830, said to be of Limerick.
Baptisms, surname **Creech**, county **Limerick**, **1825–1835**; and the **Atkinson–Creech
marriage**, Limerick, **1850–1862**, which would name both fathers.

**4. Burials or deaths** for Richard or Anne Atkinson, Limerick, **1832–1862** — anything that fixes
them in a parish.

## A caution to carry into it
Limerick is a **hard county for this period**. Many Church of Ireland registers were destroyed in the
Four Courts fire of 1922. St Mary's Cathedral is one of the parishes whose records survive, which is
the only reason Paul Cole Atkinson can be found at all. **If these searches come back empty, that may
simply be the end of the line, and the archive will record it as such** rather than reaching for a
guess.

## What to send back
Every hit, with the full transcription — parish, date, father, mother, address or townland,
occupation, sponsors or witnesses. Sponsors are often relatives and are frequently the only clue to
a wider family.
