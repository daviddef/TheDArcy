# Request — Commonwealth War Graves Commission

**What it is:** the CWGC casualty database, free, at `cwgc.org/find-records/find-war-dead/`.

**Why it matters:** it is **the** authority on where a Commonwealth war dead is buried or commemorated.
The family tree says Arthur Hartley Sneyd was "buried in France". Every indication is that he has
**no known grave** and is named on the **Villers-Bretonneux Australian National Memorial** — but that
is currently marked *inferred* on the site, from the general rule plus a secondary index, and it
should not stay that way.

## The search
Surname **Sneyd**, forename **Arthur**, war **1914–1918**, force **Army**. There should be exactly one.

## What to send back
The **cemetery or memorial name**, the **panel / plot / row / grave reference**, the age and date of
death as CWGC records them, and the **"Additional Information"** field — that last one often carries
the parents' names and address, written by the family in 1920, and would be a new primary source in
its own right.
