# Request — ScotlandsPeople: the Murdochs of St Quivox

**What it is:** `scotlandspeople.gov.uk`, the official Scottish record site. Pay-per-view credits
(a few pounds buys several images). Free index searching; you pay to see the image.

**Why it matters:** the Murdoch line is currently **documented for exactly one generation** and then
stops. We know William Murdoch was born 7 October 1856 at St Quivox and Newton, son of **Francis
Murdoch** and **Marion Watt**, and that Francis married Marion at **Avondale, Lanarkshire, on
6 May 1854**. Above Francis: nothing at all.

## The searches, in priority order

**1. Francis Murdoch's own baptism — the whole point of the exercise.**
Old Parish Registers (OPR) **Births & Baptisms**, surname **Murdoch**, forename **Francis**,
county **Ayrshire**, years **1818–1830**. He is said to be "of St Quivox, Ayr", born about 1824.
*An OPR baptism names the father and usually the mother.* **This is the record that would give the
next generation.**

**2. The marriage of 6 May 1854, Avondale, Lanarkshire.**
OPR **Marriages** (1854 is just before statutory registration began in 1855). Francis Murdoch and
Marion Watt. Pre-1855 Scottish marriage entries are terse but sometimes give both fathers, and
almost always give each party's home parish — which would confirm Francis's origin.

**3. The 1851 census, St Quivox or Avondale.**
Look for **Francis Murdoch**, and for **Marion Watt** in her father's household. A census gives
**age and parish of birth** for everyone present, which triangulates both families at once.

**4. The 1861 census** — the family with young William (b. 1856) before they emigrated. It fixes the
household on the eve of departure and gives every child's age and birthplace.

**5. Marion Watt's baptism**, OPR, Ayrshire or Lanarkshire, c. 1835–1842 — to reach the **Watt**
family, who are currently a surname and nothing more.

## What to send back
Images of anything found, plus the transcription. For the census returns, **the whole household**,
not just the named person.

## What ScotlandsPeople will *not* answer
The **emigration to Queensland**. Scotland kept no emigration records. That is a Queensland
immigration problem — assisted-passage and shipping lists at Queensland State Archives — and it is a
separate job.
